    How it works

    1. Your question is matched against German laws and official Berlin pages.
    2. The answer is written only from the passages that were found, and names them.
    3. If the sources don't cover your question, it says so instead of guessing.

    What it covers

    • Address registration (Anmeldung)
    • Student residence permits
    • Working while studying
    • Health insurance for students

    Not legal advice

    Rules and fees change. Before an important decision, check the linked law or ask the responsible office, such as the Bürgeramt or the Landesamt für Einwanderung.

    Sources: Bundesmeldegesetz, Aufenthaltsgesetz, Aufenthaltsverordnung, SGB V, LEA procedural notes, and pages from Berlin's ServicePortal, the LEA, the Federal Foreign Office and statutory health insurers.
